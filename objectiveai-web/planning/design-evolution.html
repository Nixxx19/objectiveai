Design Evolution

Breaking free from border-driven composition, one-note copper, and the old pill/round vocabulary. Everything on the copper spectrum. Side-by-side where it matters.

01 — Palette

The Copper Spectrum

Every value on the same hue line (~20°). Backgrounds are copper cooled to charcoal. Text is copper lightened to cream. Borders are copper at low alpha. Nothing arbitrary.
